🌿   Infused Jello Gummy Recipe

Infused Jello Gummies
Recipe

A simple, clean process for crafting your own island-made gummies.

Finished Product
🧪Yield: Depends on mold size
🔥Heat: Low / Medium
⏱️Set time: 20 min (fridge) or 24 hrs
🌴Difficulty: Beginner friendly
📋  Ingredients
  • 0.5 oz Gelatin 2 packets
  • 3 oz Jello 1 box, any flavor
  • ½ cup Cold Water
  • ¼ cup Corn Syrup
  • To taste Lecithin Helps bind oil & water
    Optional
  • To dose Infused MCT Oil Or other infused input — see Dosage Calculator
    Optional
  • Pinch Citric Acid For sour gummies
    Optional
📖   Instructions
  1. 1

    Mix the cold water and corn syrup together. Stir until the mixture becomes clear again.

  2. 2

    Sprinkle the gelatin into the mixture and let it bloom for 10–15 minutes, until fully hydrated and no dry clumps remain.

  3. 3

    Once bloomed, stir in the Jello packet and place on a low to medium heat source, stirring occasionally until dissolved and combined.

  4. 4

    After a few minutes on heat, stir in your lecithin and infused input of choice.

    🧮 Not sure how much infused oil to add? Use our Dosage Calculator to determine the right amount for your desired potency per gummy.
    ⚗️ Optional — Sour Gummies If you prefer sour gummies, add a pinch of citric acid at this stage.
  5. 5

    Once the mixture begins to simmer, remove from heat and allow the bubbles to settle. The froth will begin rising to the surface.

    ✨ Optional — Clarifying Carefully skim off the froth that rises to the top and discard. This may take a few minutes. Leaving it won't affect the taste — the gummies just look better without.
  6. 6

    Pour the clarified mixture into your gummy molds. Then choose your set method:

    🕐 Room temperature: Allow to sit for 24 hours, then remove from mold and let gummies continue to "sweat" and firm up.

    ❄️ Refrigerator: Set in the fridge for 20 minutes, remove from mold, then allow to continue sweating at room temp.

How to Calculate Your Dose per Piece
Step 1 — Total potency: (Total tsp of oil) × (mg per tsp of your oil)
  Example: 2 tsp × 10 mg/tsp = 20 mg total

Step 2 — Per piece: (Total mg) ÷ (Number of pieces)
  Example: 20 mg ÷ 20 pieces = 1 mg per gummy
