How to Calculate Your Dose
Step 1 — Total potency: (Amount of infused oil used) × (mg per unit of your oil)
  Example: 2 tsp × 10 mg/tsp = 20 mg total

Step 2 — Per piece/serving: (Total mg) ÷ (Number of pieces or servings)
  Example: 20 mg ÷ 20 pieces = 1 mg per piece
