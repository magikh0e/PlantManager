How the Math Works
01
Total THC Available

Grams of cannabis × THC% × 1000 = total mg of THC in the raw material.

02
Decarboxylation Loss

Heating activates THC but burns off a portion. Efficiency varies by method — typically 75–95%.

03
Infusion Efficiency

Not all activated THC binds to the oil. A good infusion captures roughly 80–90% of what's available.

04
mg Per Teaspoon

Total mg in oil ÷ total teaspoons of oil = potency per serving unit.
