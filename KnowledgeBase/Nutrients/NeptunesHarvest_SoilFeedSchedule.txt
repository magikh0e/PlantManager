                          Vegetative (1 gallon water)
wk1: 1/2oz Liquid sea weed
Watering between wk1-2: 1/2oz Liquid Seaweed

wk2: 1/2oz Fish and 1/2oz Tomato & Veg 
Watering between wk1-2: 1oz Liquid Seaweed

wk3: 1/2oz Fish and 1/2oz Tomato & veg
Watering between wk3-4: 1oz Liquid Seaweed and 1/2oz Fish

(Repeat until flowering stage)







                          Flowering (1 gallon water)

Wk4: 1/2oz Fish & 1/2oz Rose and Flowering

wk5: 1/2oz Fish & 1oz Seaweed / Fish Blend
Watering between wk5-6: 1oz Liquid Seaweed

wk6: 1/2oz Liquid Seaweed & 1oz Rose and Flowering
Watering between wk6-7: 1/2oz Fish Seaweed blend

wk7: 1/2oz Liquid Seaweed & 1/2 oz Rose and Flowering
Watering between wk7-8: 1oz Liquid Seaweed

wk8: 1/2oz Liquid Seaweed and 1/2oz Rose and Flowering
Watering between wk8-9: 1/2oz Liquid Seaweed and 5ml Mollases

Wk9: Water only until harvest, adding 7ml molasses
