Neutralizing Chloramine
for Living Soil

Breaking the Spirit Blockers · Vitamin C vs. Humic Acid

The Alchemist's Warning
"Chloramine will not leave on its own. You must break the bond alchemically, or your fortress will fall."

While traditional chlorine can be evaporated through simple aeration, chloramine (chlorine bonded with ammonia) is intentionally stabilized so it will not off-gas. Pouring chloramine-treated water into your fabric pots will instantly burn the delicate fungal hyphae and strip the life from your Microbial Fortress.

⚡
Method 01 The Ascorbic Strike — Vitamin C

Ascorbic acid (Vitamin C) is the fastest and cleanest way to break the chloramine bond. It triggers an instant chemical reaction that reduces chloramine into harmless chloride and a tiny amount of plant-available nitrogen from the ammonia component.

💊 The Input

Use pure Ascorbic Acid powder or Sodium Ascorbate. Avoid dietary vitamin C tablets that contain fillers, artificial colors, or flavorings — these additives can harm soil biology. Food-grade ascorbic acid powder is available at most health food stores and online.

⚖️ The Alchemical Ratio

It takes very little to achieve neutralization. The standard dose is 50mg of Ascorbic Acid per 1 gallon of water. For a 50-gallon reservoir, you need only about 2.5 grams (roughly ½ teaspoon) of ascorbic acid powder. A little goes a long way.

⏱️ The Reaction Time

The reaction is nearly instantaneous. Once stirred vigorously into the reservoir, the water is completely safe for the living soil food web within 3 to 5 minutes. This is the fastest neutralization method available.

⚠️ The Drawback

Ascorbic acid mildly lowers the pH of the water (typically by 0.2–0.5 points) and degrades rapidly when exposed to light and air. You must use the treated water within 24 hours — after that, the protective effect fades and any remaining chloramine can reactivate.

Quick Dose Reference: 50mg per gallon = roughly 1 pinch per gallon, ¼ tsp per 25 gallons, or ½ tsp per 50 gallons. When in doubt, slightly over-dosing is safe — excess Vitamin C simply breaks down harmlessly.
🌊
🛡️
Method 02 The Humic Bond — Humic Acid

If Vitamin C is the swift strike, Humic Acid is the nourishing shield. Humic acid is a complex organic compound extracted from ancient compressed organic matter (leonardite). It not only neutralizes chloramine but actively feeds the soil biology.

🧪 The Input

Use a high-quality Dry Soluble Humic Acid powder or a pure Liquid Humic Extract. Source matters — look for products derived from leonardite (ancient plant matter), not lignite coal. The best humic products are 70%+ humic/fulvic acid content.

⚖️ The Alchemical Ratio

Dry Powder: Dissolve ¼ teaspoon per 5 gallons of water. Liquid Extract: Mix roughly 5ml (1 teaspoon) per 1 gallon of water. The water will turn a dark amber/brown color — this is normal and indicates the humic compounds are fully dissolved.

⏱️ The Reaction Time

Humic acid requires more time to fully bind and neutralize the chloramine molecules. Add it to your reservoir, agitate the water vigorously, and let it rest for 30 to 60 minutes before watering. The binding is chemical, not just dilution — patience is required.

✨ The Double Benefit

Unlike Vitamin C, Humic Acid leaves behind a rich carbon source that stimulates mycorrhizal fungi expansion and helps chelate heavy metals in the soil, making bound minerals available to plant roots. It's a powerful double-duty input — neutralization plus soil conditioning in one step.

TRM Suggests: Use Vitamin C for speed (when you need water immediately) and Humic Acid for depth (when you can wait 30–60 minutes and want the soil-conditioning bonus). Many growers alternate — Vitamin C for daily watering, Humic Acid once a week for the added biology boost.

The Protocol The Neutralization Ritual — Step by Step

To ensure absolute purity before the water touches the soil, follow this protocol in your Alchemical Sanctuary.

Step 1 Fill the Vault

Fill your primary reservoir with municipal tap water. Use a dark, opaque tank to prevent algae growth. Note the volume — you'll need it for precise dosing.

Step 2 Aerate (Optional but Recommended)

Drop an air stone into the water for 30 minutes. While this won't remove the chloramine, it will off-gas any volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and oxygenate the water — boosting dissolved oxygen levels for healthier microbial activity downstream.

Step 3 Apply the Catalyst

Choose your method: the Ascorbic Strike (½ tsp per 50 gal for immediate use) or the Humic Bond (¼ tsp dry per 5 gal for soil conditioning). Measure precisely — over-dosing Vitamin C is harmless, but over-dosing Humic Acid can temporarily drop pH below the target range.

Step 4 The Vortex

Vigorously stir the water to ensure the catalyst is perfectly homogenized. If using an air stone, it will help distribute the compound. For Humic Acid, you may see the water turn dark amber — this is correct. No clumps should remain.

Step 5 The Rest

Wait the required time: 3–5 minutes for Vitamin C or 30–60 minutes for Humic Acid. You can verify with a total chlorine test strip — it should read zero. If any reading remains, add a small additional dose and retest.

Step 6 Hydrate the Fortress

Deliver the purified liquid mana to your living soil via your bottom-watering Aquifer system or gentle top-watering. The water is now safe for the entire microbial food web — fungi, bacteria, protozoa, and nematodes will thrive rather than perish.

Island Intel Maui County Water & Island-Specific Tips
🚰 Maui Uses Chloramine

Maui County Department of Water Supply uses chloramine as its primary disinfectant. This means off-gassing with an air stone will NOT work for Maui tap water. You must use Vitamin C or Humic Acid treatment every single time. No exceptions.

🌧️ The Catchment Alternative

Many upcountry Maui homes run on rainwater catchment, which is naturally chloramine-free. If you have access to catchment water, it's the ideal grow water — just add a sediment/carbon filter and an air stone. No chemical treatment needed.

🧪 Test Your Source

Pick up total chlorine test strips (not just "free chlorine" — you need the combined reading) from any pool supply store in Kahului. Test before and after treatment to confirm neutralization. A $10 pack of strips lasts hundreds of tests and provides peace of mind for your fortress.

🛒 Where to Source on Maui

Ascorbic Acid: Mana Foods (Pāʻia), Down to Earth (Kahului), or order bulk online. Humic Acid: Maui hydroponics shops carry quality brands, or order leonardite-derived products online. Avoid cheap "humic" products that are actually dyed water with minimal active content.

⚠️

Never skip treatment: Even a single watering with untreated Maui tap water can set your living soil back weeks. Chloramine doesn't just reduce microbial populations — it selectively kills the most beneficial organisms first (mycorrhizal fungi and nitrogen-fixing bacteria), leaving behind only the hardiest, often pathogenic, survivors. One slip can shift your entire soil ecosystem.
