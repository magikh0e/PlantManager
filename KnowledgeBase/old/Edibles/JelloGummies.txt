Ingredients:
.5oz Gelatin (2 packets)
3oz Jello (1 box)
1/2 cup cold water
1/4 cup Corn Syrup


Steps:
1. Mix the water and corn syrup togeter. Stir until the mxiture becomes clear again.
2. Sprinkle the gelatin into this mix, wait 10-15 mins for it to fully bloom is usually enough.
3. Once bloomed, mix in the Jello packet and place on LOW/MED heat source, stirring occasionally.

- After a few mins you can stir in Lecithin & whatever infused input you wish to use. (I use infused MCT oil..)
 Optional: If you prefer sour gummies, you can add citric acid during this step.
- Once the mixture begins to simmer, remove the pot from heat source and allow the bubbles to start rising.
 Optional: Carefully scrape off the froth that rises to the top of the mixture and discard. It may take a few minutes 
           for all of the froth to rise up. Wont hurt anything leaving it, the gummies just look better without.
4. Once you have clarified (removing the froth) the gelatin or not, you can now pour into your molds.. 
   Allow to sit roomn temps 24 hours or straight into the fridge for 20 mins.
   After the 24 hours in room temps, or 20 mins in the fridge, remove gummies from mold and allow to tcontinue sweating.
