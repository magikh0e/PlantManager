v2.19.1 — 2026-03-11
--------------------
FEATURE: Plant Nicknames & Auto-Numbering

  Differentiate multiple plants of the same strain with optional nicknames and
  automatic plant numbering. When you add a second plant with the same strain name,
  both are auto-assigned #1 and #2. Set a custom nickname (e.g. "Mom A", "North Tent")
  that appears across all dropdowns, feeding logs, tasks, charts, calendar, notifications,
  and the FleetView table. Display format: "Blue Dream — Yoda" (with nickname) or
  "Blue Dream #2" (auto-number). Plant cards show nickname/number below strain name.
  FleetView gains a sortable Label column. Search includes nicknames.

FEATURE: Star Wars Name Suggestions

  Click the dice button (🎲) next to the Nickname field when adding or editing a plant
  to randomly pick from 66 Star Wars character names (Luke, Leia, Yoda, Grogu, Ahsoka,
  Mando, etc.). The picker avoids names already in use by active plants. Click again
  for a different suggestion, or type any custom name you prefer.

POLISH: Plant Card UI Refinement

  Plant cards now display a stage-themed accent stripe across the top, color-coded
  by growth stage: lime/green for seedling and vegetative, gold/orange for
  flowering, violet for mature, muted gray for declining. Cards lift 3px and gain
  a subtle cyan glow shadow on hover. Stripe brightens to full opacity on hover.

POLISH: Action Button Hierarchy

  The 19-button action grid on each plant card has been condensed to 5 primary
  actions (+ Feed, Edit, + Photo, + Task, Health) with the remaining 14 actions
  collapsed into a "More" dropdown menu (Clone, IPM Log, Identify Issue, Harvest,
  QR Tag, Timeline, Dry-Back, Lineage, Plant Report, Compare, Trichome, Training
  Log, Breeding, Archive). The + Feed button is now styled as the primary
  cyan-gradient action since feeding is the most common task. Archive is the last
  item in the More menu, separated and tinted red. Click outside the menu to
  close it.

POLISH: Inline Feed CTA on Dashboard

  Feeding Alerts on the dashboard now render as compact rows with an inline
  "+ Feed" button per plant, opening the feeding log directly without needing
  to navigate to the plant card first.

FIX: Undefined Health Badge

  Plants missing a healthStatus field no longer render a literal
  "undefined" badge. The badge is now suppressed entirely when the field is
  empty.

POLISH: Custom Scrollbar Theming

  Page scrollbars now match the dark theme — thin cyan-tinted thumbs that
  intensify on hover, transparent track. Firefox uses scrollbar-color, Chromium
  uses ::-webkit-scrollbar pseudo-elements. Light theme gets a darker cyan
  variant.

POLISH: SVG Dashboard Stat Icons

  The 10 emoji icons on dashboard stat cards (Mother Plants, Flowering, Active
  Clones, Environments, Tasks Due, Health Overview, Active Issues, Env Alerts,
  Strains, Total Yield) have been replaced with custom inline SVG icons that
  inherit the card's accent color (cyan, gold, green, orange, pink). Icons
  scale and slightly rotate on hover for tactile feedback.

POLISH: Stat-Card Hover Lift

  Stat cards now lift 3px on hover with a soft cyan-tinted glow shadow,
  matching the plant card treatment.

POLISH: Color-Coded Plant Tags

  The pill-tags on plant cards (strain type, breeder, source) now have distinct
  visual treatments: strain type is colored by indica/sativa/hybrid/ruderalis
  (violet/orange/green/tan), breeder is cyan italic, source is gold uppercase.
  Easier to scan a card at a glance.

POLISH: Compact Quick Add Row

  Dashboard Quick Add buttons are now 25% shorter and use a tighter font weight
  to reduce visual weight when 9-10 buttons stack horizontally. Buttons lift on
  hover.

POLISH: Tab Bar Refinement

  The 13-tab navigation bar has been tightened (smaller font, less padding,
  62px min-width) so all tabs fit comfortably at 1280px+ viewports. The right-
  edge fade mask now only appears when the bar is actually scrollable (narrow
  viewports). Previously the fade always cut into the rightmost "Readings" tab
  even on wide screens.

POLISH: Collapsible Hero Banner

  The Kānehiwa hero illustration now defaults to a 280px-tall compact mode
  (focal-zoomed on the upper portion of the artwork) instead of consuming the
  full above-the-fold area. A small expand/collapse button in the corner toggles
  between compact and full views, with the choice remembered per-user via the
  heroExpanded setting. The existing show/hide toggle in the header still works.

SEO: Static H1, noscript Fallback, Image Performance Hints

  Three on-page SEO refinements: (1) the header title is now a real <h1> element
  instead of a <span>, giving non-JS-executing crawlers (Bing, social scrapers,
  archive.org) a real document heading. Visual styling unchanged. (2) Added a
  <noscript> block at the top of <body> with a brief description and links to
  the marketing site and help guide for visitors with JavaScript disabled.
  (3) The Kānehiwa hero image and the header logo now carry fetchpriority="high",
  decoding="async", and loading="eager" — improving Largest Contentful Paint
  (LCP) timing and Core Web Vitals scores.

DOCS: FAQ Section in PlantManagerHelp.html

  Added a Frequently Asked Questions section with 10 expandable Q&A pairs
  covering the most-asked topics: data privacy & storage, browser cache loss,
  offline support, cross-device sync, plant differentiation (nicknames &
  auto-numbering), export encryption, browser support, harvest estimate
  accuracy, and QR plant tag printing. Built with native HTML <details>/
  <summary> for keyboard accessibility and screen-reader compatibility.
  Distinctive styling: gold "Q" badges, Cinzel small-caps questions, cyan
  expand indicators that rotate to × when open, glow shadow on expanded items.
  Each item slides in on expand. Mobile-responsive padding at <600px.

  Added FAQPage schema.org JSON-LD to the <head> with all 10 Question/Answer
  pairs structured for Google rich results — these can appear as expandable
  FAQ accordions directly in search engine result pages, improving CTR.

  Table of Contents updated with a new "Frequently Asked Questions" entry.


v2.18.1 — 2026-03-12
--------------------
FEATURE: Encrypted Exports (Optional)

  Optionally encrypt data backups with a password using AES-256-GCM encryption.
  Key derivation uses PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and 600,000 iterations for brute-force
  resistance. Toggle encryption on/off via gear menu → Export Encryption. When
  enabled, Export Data prompts for a password with confirmation and strength
  indicator, then exports a .encrypted.json file. On import, encrypted files are
  auto-detected by their TRM_ENC_V1 header and prompt for the decryption password.
  Unencrypted backups are always accepted regardless of the setting. Password
  recovery is not possible — a warning is shown during export.

FEATURE: Nutrient Availability / pH Chart Reference

  Interactive visual chart showing nutrient availability across the pH spectrum
  (3.0–9.0). 14 nutrients (N, P, K, Ca, Mg, S, Fe, Mn, B, Cu, Zn, Mo, Cl, Ni)
  displayed as horizontal SVG bars with optimal (green) and reduced (gold) ranges.
  Draggable pH slider highlights current position with cyan indicator line.
  Toggle between Soil/Organic and Hydro/Coco modes for different availability
  ranges. Auto-reads latest logged pH from environment readings or feeding data.
  Status summary shows locked out, reduced, and available nutrient counts.

FEATURE: CO₂ Calculator & Tracking

  Two-part feature: (1) CO₂ calculator for sealed rooms — enter room dimensions
  (L×W×H), current CO₂, target PPM, and injection rate (CFH) to calculate CO₂
  needed (cu ft) and injection time (minutes). Growth stage presets: Ambient
  (400), Veg (800), Early Flower (1200), Peak Flower (1500), Late Flower (1000).
  Save defaults for reuse. (2) CO₂ PPM field added to environment readings —
  log CO₂ levels alongside temperature, humidity, pH. Displayed on reading cards,
  sparkline in trends table, and CO₂ trend chart in the calculator modal.

FEATURE: Batch Feeding / Bulk Operations

  Log one feeding event for multiple plants at once. Opens a modal with an
  environment filter dropdown, selectable plant checkbox grid (strain, stage,
  environment shown per card), Select All / Deselect buttons with count badge,
  and full feeding form (nutrient, amount, unit, water volume, pH in/out,
  EC in/out, notes). On submit, creates individual feeding entries for each
  selected plant. Accessible from Feeding tab toolbar ("Batch Feed" button),
  gear menu (Growing group), and dashboard quick actions.
